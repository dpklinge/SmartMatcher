Subject: HCMST 2017–2020–2022 offline relationship-outcome benchmark

Dear Professor Rosenfeld,

We are using the public HCMST 2017–2020–2022 v2.2 dataset for an offline feasibility benchmark supporting research for a relationship-matching application under development.

The benchmark predicts reported breakup by the 2020 follow-up among respondents partnered in 2017, with a separate respondent-rated quality proxy. It compares baseline profile variables with existing-relationship context and relationship quality. Nonresponse and partner deaths are excluded from breakup labels, and continued relationships must explicitly refer to the 2017 partner. We recognize that these data do not validate matching strangers or support a causal compatibility claim.

We cite Rosenfeld, Thomas, and Hausen (2023), do not attempt identification, and do not redistribute the raw data. The models remain outside the application's live matching system. We would also appreciate guidance about any additional conditions for future commercial model use; no such deployment is part of this benchmark.

Please include this work in the record of projects using HCMST. We can provide a public citation or publication reference if one becomes available.
