## Interpretation

For breakup, the test AUROC is 0.737 for profile proxies, 0.907 for existing-relationship context, and 0.930 when current quality is also available. These models address continuation of established couples, not the benefit of recommending a new pair. Only 37 breakup events occur in the test split; uncertainty is material and no paired significance claim is made. The profile model also includes ages and employment, so its performance cannot be attributed to shared values or similarity.

Of 2,862 currently partnered baseline participants, 1,146 have no 2020 response, 41 report partner death, and 4 have inconsistent/uncertain end status. These 1,191 participants are excluded from both targets. None are imputed as relationship failures. This substantial attrition and the pandemic horizon limit transfer to new app users.

Higher AUROC/AP and lower Brier are better. The prior model provides a base-rate reference. Bootstrap intervals resample test participants (500 draws); they are not survey-design confidence intervals. Weighted metrics use Stanford’s 2020 attrition-adjusted weights as a sensitivity check; training and selection are unweighted. Results do not eliminate nonresponse bias.

Full metrics, calibration bins, selected regularization, source hashes and environment versions: `research-artifacts/hcmst/results.json` (local, ignored). No model or participant predictions are exported to the application.
